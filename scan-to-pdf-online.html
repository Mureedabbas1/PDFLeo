Create a PDF from Scanned Pages

Scanning paper documents into PDF creates a convenient digital copy of receipts, notes, forms, and records. A scan is often an image rather than searchable text, so OCR may be needed if you want to search or copy words.

Open Create a PDF from Scanned Pages Tool

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Prepare the paper pages and place them in the correct order.
  2. Open Scan to PDF and choose the supported scan or image input workflow.
  3. Adjust page orientation and framing using the options available.
  4. Create and download the PDF.
  5. Open the result and check page order, clarity, cropped edges, and whether every page is included.

Tips for better results

  • Use even lighting and avoid shadows across the text.
  • Keep the camera or scanner steady and capture the full page.
  • For searchable text, run OCR on the finished scan if needed.
Important: Keep an untouched original and inspect the result before sharing. For sensitive or official documents, use a workflow appropriate to your requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Will a scanned PDF automatically contain searchable text?

Not always; many scans contain page images only and require OCR.

How do I improve scan quality?

Use good lighting, straight alignment, clear focus, and enough resolution.

Can I combine several scanned pages?

Use the available multi-page workflow and verify the final page order.

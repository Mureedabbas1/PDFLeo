Extract Pages from a PDF

Extracting pages creates a separate PDF containing the pages you need, such as a single invoice, selected chapter, or attachment from a larger report. It is useful when you want to keep the original document intact.

Open Extract Pages Tool

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open Extract PDF Pages and upload your source document.
  2. Enter or select the page numbers using the tool's supported format.
  3. Double-check the selected pages against the viewer before running the task.
  4. Download the extracted PDF.
  5. Open it and confirm the page order and content are correct.

Tips for better results

  • Printed page labels may not match PDF page positions.
  • Keep the original if you may need other pages later.
  • If you need every page as its own file, check the Split PDF tool instead.
Before sharing: Keep an untouched copy of important files and inspect the downloaded output. PDF features, page structure, and output quality can vary.

Frequently asked questions

Does extraction remove pages from my original?

No, it should create a separate output; keep the source file.

Can I extract non-consecutive pages?

Use the selection format supported by the tool and verify the output after processing.

Why are the page numbers different?

PDF viewer positions count physical pages, while a document may display its own printed numbering.

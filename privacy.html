Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

PDFLeo provides browser-based document tools. This policy explains, at a high level, how this website handles information.

Files you select

The PDF processing features currently available on this website are designed to process selected files locally in your browser. PDFLeo does not intentionally upload those selected documents to a PDFLeo server. Your browser may store temporary data while the page is open.

Usage and technical information

The hosting provider and any services added to this website in the future may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and request logs to deliver and protect the site. The PDF processing library is loaded from a third-party content delivery network.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This initial version does not intentionally set PDFLeo advertising or analytics cookies. If analytics, advertising, or Google AdSense is added later, this policy should be updated to explain the relevant cookies, data use, consent options, and third-party providers before those services are enabled.

Children

This website is a general-purpose utility service and is not specifically directed to children.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as features change. The updated date above will be revised when changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@pdfleo.online. This address is a proposed contact address and must be configured before relying on it.

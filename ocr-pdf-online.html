OCR a Scanned PDF into Searchable Text

Optical character recognition (OCR) attempts to recognize text inside scanned pages and photos. Accuracy depends on resolution, contrast, language, skew, and page layout; always proofread important names, numbers, and wording.

Open OCR a Scanned PDF into Searchable Text Tool

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Start with the clearest available scan and keep an original copy.
  2. Open OCR PDF and select the scanned document.
  3. Run OCR using the language and options supported by the tool.
  4. Download the output and search for a few words that appear on different pages.
  5. Proofread names, dates, totals, tables, and any text that will be reused.

Tips for better results

  • Straighten tilted pages and improve contrast before processing where possible.
  • Handwriting, tiny text, stamps, and multi-column layouts are harder to recognize.
  • OCR output may add a text layer without reproducing the original layout perfectly.
Important: Keep an untouched original and inspect the result before sharing. For sensitive or official documents, use a workflow appropriate to your requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Does OCR guarantee perfect recognition?

No. OCR can confuse similar characters and may miss text in poor scans.

Can OCR recognize handwriting?

Handwriting recognition is harder and may not be supported.

Why is some text missing?

The scan may be too faint, low-resolution, rotated, or outside the supported language.

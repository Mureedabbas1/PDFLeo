Crop Margins from PDF Pages

Cropping can remove excess margins or focus attention on a section of a page. Cropping changes what is visible in the page view; do not assume it securely deletes underlying sensitive content unless the tool explicitly removes that content and you verify the output.

Open Crop Margins from PDF Pages Tool

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open Crop PDF Pages and select your document.
  2. Choose the crop area or settings offered by the tool.
  3. Apply the crop and download the result.
  4. Review pages with different sizes or orientations to ensure content is not cut off.
  5. Check the output in another PDF viewer and compare it with the original.

Tips for better results

  • Keep a copy of the original because a crop may hide content you later need.
  • Use extra margin around signatures, footnotes, and page numbers.
  • Do not use cropping as a substitute for redaction of confidential information.
Important: Keep an untouched original and inspect the result before sharing. For sensitive or official documents, use a workflow appropriate to your requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping permanently erase content outside the visible area?

Not necessarily. Cropping may change the visible page area without securely deleting underlying content.

Can I crop every page the same way?

That depends on the tool's controls; mixed page sizes may need extra review.

Can cropping hide confidential details?

Do not rely on it for confidentiality. Use a verified redaction workflow and inspect the result.

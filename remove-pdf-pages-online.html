Remove Pages from a PDF

Remove blank sheets, duplicates, or outdated sections before sharing a document. Work on a copy and verify the remaining page sequence—especially when the PDF includes printed page numbers, appendices, or signatures.

Open Remove Pages Tool

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open Remove PDF Pages and select the document.
  2. Identify the pages to remove using the tool's page-selection controls.
  3. Review the selected page numbers before processing.
  4. Create and download the edited PDF.
  5. Check the beginning, end, page count, and any references to removed sections.

Tips for better results

  • Viewer page numbers may differ from printed page numbers on the page itself.
  • Removing pages from a signed PDF may invalidate its digital signature.
  • If you need to save selected pages instead, use Extract PDF Pages.
Before sharing: Keep an untouched copy of important files and inspect the downloaded output. PDF features, page structure, and output quality can vary.

Frequently asked questions

Can I recover removed pages from the output?

The output may not include them; keep the original PDF as a backup.

Does removing a page hide its information securely?

It removes pages from the resulting document, but always inspect the output and metadata before sharing sensitive files.

What if I selected the wrong page?

Return to the original copy and repeat the operation.

Redact Sensitive Information in a PDF

Redaction is intended to remove sensitive content before a document is shared. PDFLeo's current Redact PDF workflow rasterizes every page and removes selectable text from the output; this changes the document and is not a certified redaction workflow. For legal or highly sensitive files, use a verified specialist redaction tool.

Open Redact Sensitive Information in a PDF Tool

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Keep an untouched original and identify every sensitive item that must be removed.
  2. Open PDFLeo Redact PDF and select the document only if its limitations are acceptable for your use case.
  3. Apply the supported redaction process and download the output.
  4. Inspect every page visually at high zoom, including headers, footers, annotations, and attachments where relevant.
  5. Search or copy text from the output and check metadata before sharing; use a specialist tool for high-risk documents.

Tips for better results

  • Never cover text with a black rectangle and assume the underlying text has been deleted.
  • Rasterized pages can reduce accessibility, searchability, and text quality.
  • For legal, medical, financial, or regulated records, confirm that the chosen workflow meets your compliance requirements.
Important: Keep an untouched original and inspect the result before sharing. For sensitive or official documents, use a workflow appropriate to your requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Is a black box over text enough?

No. Visual covering may leave the original text extractable.

Does PDFLeo provide certified redaction?

No. The current tool rasterizes pages and is not a certified specialist redaction workflow.

Should I use it for highly sensitive documents?

Use a verified specialist redaction tool and inspect the final file before disclosure.

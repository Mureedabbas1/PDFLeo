Bulk QR Code Generator

Paste one URL or text item per line. The generator creates a separate PNG for each line and bundles the files into a ZIP download.

For best results, use short, valid URLs or text. Each non-empty line becomes one PNG.

How to use Bulk QR Code Generator

  1. Enter the URL or text, or upload the image where requested.
  2. Choose your preferred size and colors.
  3. Generate and download your QR code.
  4. Scan the final PNG with a phone before sharing or printing.

Privacy and safety

QR generation happens locally in your browser. The text and logo you enter are not sent to this website's server by this page. The page loads QR and ZIP libraries from a content delivery network. A QR code does not verify that a destination is safe, so check links before printing or sharing.

Frequently asked questions

Is this tool free?

Yes. You can use this QR generator without creating an account.

Will the QR code expire?

The QR code stores the supplied text or URL. It does not expire, but a destination website may later change or become unavailable.

Should I test the code before printing?

Yes. Always scan-test the downloaded PNG on a phone and check the destination.

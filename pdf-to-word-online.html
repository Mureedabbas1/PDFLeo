Convert PDF to Word Online

Converting a PDF to Word helps you reuse text in a report, letter, or draft. PDFs preserve fixed page layouts, while Word documents are editable, so columns, tables, spacing, and fonts may need manual correction after conversion.

Open Convert PDF to Word Tool

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open PDF to Word and choose a text-based PDF where possible.
  2. Run the conversion and download the DOCX file.
  3. Open the DOCX in a compatible word processor.
  4. Review headings, line breaks, tables, images, footnotes, and page breaks.
  5. Compare key names, dates, and figures against the original PDF before using the file.

Tips for better results

  • Scanned pages may need OCR before their text can be edited.
  • Complex layouts may convert into separate text blocks or shift alignment.
  • Keep the source PDF as the visual reference.
Quality check: Keep an original copy and inspect the exported file. Conversion results depend on file structure, fonts, scans, and supported features.

Frequently asked questions

Will the Word file look identical to the PDF?

Not always; PDF-to-Word conversion may change layout and spacing.

Can I convert a scanned PDF?

A scan usually needs OCR to recognize text before it can be edited.

Are tables always preserved?

No; inspect and correct table rows, columns, and totals manually.

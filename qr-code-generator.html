Where can you use QR codes?

QR codes are useful on business cards, menus, product packaging, posters, event invitations, flyers and signs. A website QR code lets people open your page without typing its address.

Privacy and safety

This generator creates the QR code in your browser. The text you enter is not submitted to a QR-generation service by this page. The QR library itself is loaded from a public content delivery network. A QR code does not verify whether a destination is safe, so check every link before printing or sharing.

Frequently asked questions

Is the QR code free?

Yes. You can generate and download QR codes without creating an account.

Do QR codes expire?

A static QR code does not expire by itself. It stores the content you entered, so a printed website QR code will keep pointing to the same URL unless that URL changes or stops working.

Which size should I choose?

512 pixels works for many digital uses. Choose 1024 pixels for larger print layouts, and always test the final printed size.
